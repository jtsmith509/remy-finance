### 1. Bid/ask sizes: PASS
- Fields: `bid, bid_size, bid_exchange, bid_condition, ask, ask_size, ask_exchange, ask_condition, timestamp`.
- Displayed sizes are in contracts. Median sizes at 10:00 were 4 (SPY), 40–96 (AAPL) and 47–110 (KO). SPY's small displayed sizes matter for FILL-1 (DR-0006): fill rates on SPY may be low at the \$2,000 sizing.
- Quotes with zero on one side carry `bid=0`, `bid_size=0`. The adapter must treat them as one-sided, which makes the candidate `UNPRICEABLE` (R0.7).

### 2. Underlying prices: PASS after upgrading to Stock Standard (DR-0013)
**Rerun on 2026-11-08 at 06:33 UTC, after the upgrade.** `stock/at_time/quote` now returns the underlying NBBO with sizes at 10:00:

| | Bid / ask (sizes) | Midpoint | Greeks `underlying_price` @ 10:00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPY | 740.50 / 740.71 (120 × 80) | 740.605 | 740.60 |
| AAPL | 342.89 / 343.00 (200 × 280) | 342.945 | 342.94 |
| KO | 94.52 / 94.58 (100 × 200) | 94.55 | 94.55 |

- **The look-ahead question is resolved.** The greeks `underlying_price` stamped at a minute equals the underlying **NBBO midpoint at that instant**. It does not come from data later in that minute. This is inferred from three exact matches.
- **New caveat:** the stock `at_time` row's `timestamp` is the *requested* time (`10:00:00.000`), not the quote's own time. Option `at_time` rows carry real quote times, such as `09:59:55.898`. Staleness of the underlying quote therefore can't be measured from the data. We rely on ThetaData's documented behaviour (the last quote at or before that time), backed by the midpoint match above. The adapter records the requested time in provenance and flags `quote_time_is_request_time`.

The findings below come from the first run on the FREE tier:
- **Have:**
  - `option/history/greeks/first_order` (Options Standard) returns `underlying_price` on a 1-minute grid. For example, SPY was 740.41 @ 09:59, 740.60 @ 10:00 and 741.01 @ 10:01.
  - `stock/history/eod` (FREE) returns `close`.
- **Lack:** the underlying **bid/ask and sizes** at 10:00 and 15:45. These are needed for `UnderlyingQuote` and for ASSIGN-1 forced stock closes (DR-0009).
- **Look-ahead caution:** `underlying_timestamp` sits exactly on the minute (`10:00:00.000`). It's undocumented whether that value is the bar *start* (built from data in 10:00:00–10:00:59, which would be look-ahead for a 10:00 decision) or the last value *at* 10:00:00. Until that's established, the adapter must use the bar stamped **one minute earlier** (09:59) for a 10:00 decision.
- **Stock EOD caveat (EXP-1, DR-0010):**
  - The `bid`/`ask` in the EOD row are taken at report time (17:15 ET), after the close. For SPY they were 735.81/735.90 against a close of 735.29, so they are **not** a closing NBBO and must not be used to close stock.
  - `close` looks like the regular-session close, but a spot-check against the exchange's official close is still needed.
- **Options:**
  - **(a)** Stock **Value**. Gives historical underlying NBBO for replay, but live stock data is 15-minute delayed, which doesn't work for prospective snapshots.
  - **(b)** Stock **Standard**. Real-time (Nasdaq Basic, not the consolidated NBBO) plus history.
  - **(c)** Stay on FREE. Use the previous minute's greeks `underlying_price` and approximate the stock bid/ask conservatively for ASSIGN-1 closes. This changes a handoff rule, so it needs a new DR.
- **Recommendation:** (b) if the cost is acceptable; otherwise (c) with a DR.

### 3. Dividends and corporate actions: FAIL, so a supplementary source is needed
- **What's missing:** ThetaData v3 has no endpoint for them. ASSIGN-1's dividend rule needs the ex-date, the amount and when the dividend became public. Corporate actions also change option deliverables, and non-standard deliverables must be excluded.
- **Dividends:** proposed source is a provider whose records carry a **declaration date**, such as Polygon.io / Massive reference dividends or Nasdaq dividend history.
- **Conservative `public_time`:** the declaration date at 16:00 ET, usable from the next snapshot.
- **Audit-grade option:** SEC EDGAR 8-K acceptance timestamps.
- **Corporate actions and adjusted contracts:** OCC Information Memos (with publication dates). Also exclude any contract whose OSI root has a numeric suffix (e.g. `AAPL1`).
- To be fixed in a new DR once Josh chooses.

### 4. Time-of-day retrieval: works one day per request; look-ahead test pending
- **Request shape (measured):** `at_time/quote` with `expiration=*` needs `start_date == end_date`. A replay day therefore costs 50 names × 4 times (2 snapshots + 2 execution quotes) = **200 requests**. At about 3 s mean and 4 concurrent, that's roughly 2–3 minutes per replay day, or about 1 hour per replay month.
- **`max_dte` is ignored** by `at_time/quote`: the rows include 2027 and 2029 expirations, and row counts with and without it are identical. DTE and strike bounds (DR-0008) must be applied in our own code.
- **Prospective snapshots:** `snapshot/quote` takes 0.1–0.2 s per name, so a 50-name live snapshot takes well under 30 s.
- **Rerun pending:** the rerun checks every row's timestamp against the requested **date and time**. It reports how old the quotes are and how many came from a prior day, and checks 10:00, 10:05, 15:45 and 15:50.
- **Script bug fixed:** the original staleness test compared clock times only and ignored the date, so it would have missed a later-date quote at an earlier clock time. That's now fixed and unit-tested.

### 5. Expired contracts: PASS
`list/expirations` and `list/strikes` cover expired expirations back to 2012. Historical quotes for a month-old expired chain come back with sizes. `list/contracts/quote?date=D` reconstructs the contract set as of a past date, which is what R0.5 and replay need.
