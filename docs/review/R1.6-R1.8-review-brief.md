## 3. Invariants to attack (most important first)

1. **No look-ahead in the scheduler or replay.**
   - Recommendations may use only the stored decision board.
   - Fills and management may use only that slot's execution board.
   - Settlement needs an official close that is public (`official_close_as_of`, 17:15 ET).
   - Can a tick, a re-run or a replay ever use a board or a close stamped after the decision it serves?
   - Check the execution-start rule in `tick.py` (max(scheduled +5 min, decision `as_of` + 5 min)) against the engine's window check.
   - Check the replay's simulated clock (`replay.py`). Can a simulated `receipt_time` fall after its execution board, or before its decision?
2. **Idempotence and crash safety.**
   - Re-running a tick or a replay must write nothing new.
   - Construct a crash between any two steps, for example after recommendations but before the run log, or after `EXIT_SUBMITTED` but before its fill. Does a re-run then duplicate, skip or change an event?
   - The per-day cap counts only *earlier* snapshots (`underlyings_picked_today(before=...)`). Is that enough for a re-run to reproduce identical picks?
   - Daily markers are only an optimisation. Can a marker ever be written before the work it marks is complete?
3. **Slot finality and gaps** (`tick.py` `_slot`, `_gap`).
   - Every slot must end as exactly one of stored or gap: on a missed window, a failed capture, a capture that overruns, a decision gap that should force an execution gap, or two ticks racing.
   - Is a gap ever recorded over a stored snapshot, or a snapshot written after its gap?
4. **Integrity rules.**
   - A dirty tree must skip that slot's recommendations without blocking management (`DirtyTreeError`).
   - A late receipt must give an unfilled entry with an accurate reason.
   - Can uncommitted code ever produce a prospective recommendation?
   - Does the `+dirty` replay path leak into prospective records?
5. **Export and backup.**
   - `push_export` is commit and push in a separate checkout. Can a failed push lose rows, double-export them, or set the marker without a push?
   - Does `registry verify --export-dir` still catch truncation once the export lives in a real git checkout?
   - The backup runs once per session. Is the timing (17:30 ET, latest session, weekends) right?
6. **Secrets.**
   - The Massive key comes from the environment or the Keychain (`secrets.py`). Can it reach a log, a failure record, a notification, the plist, `doctor` output or an exception message?
7. **Reports against EVAL-1** (`reports/`).
   - Check each against the frozen protocol:
     - CRPS (quantile form);
     - the binned log score (floor 1e-4);
     - PIT (linear interpolation, deterministic draws in point masses and beyond the outer quantiles);
     - coverage;
     - corrected versus original P&L;
     - the 61-day eligibility rule;
     - drawdown per close day;
     - grouping by engine and version.
   - Is anything that EVAL-1 reserves for confirmatory analysis (p-values for P1 or P2) ever printed?
   - Do prospective and replay ever mix?
8. **Measured power inputs** (`reports/measured.py`).
   - The design effect is a cluster-robust (CR1) variance over the iid variance, with clusters by ISO week of expiration.
   - Is the formula right, and is the implied ρ the correct inverse of the Kish design effect?
   - Edge cases: one cluster, zero variance, very few trades.
9. **R0 amendment (MOVEGEN-2).**
   - `_shapes` in `movegen/generator.py` builds:
     - straddles at every strike quoted on both sides;
     - strangles from OTM put × OTM call pairs;
     - long iron condors with OTM inner longs and wings one width out;
     - long iron butterflies centred at the strike nearest spot.
   - Are the sides, strikes and wings right for every band width and lattice? Can any of them produce undefined risk, a duplicate, or a structure that isn't a debit at realistic prices?
   - Is MOVEGEN-1's output (and config hash) byte-identical to before?
   - Do ASSIGN-1 and EXP-1 treat the short wings exactly like other short legs? There is an end-to-end test in `tests/unit/execution/test_engine.py`.

## 4. Already settled (don't re-report unless the implementation is wrong)
- **Scheduler design:** one idempotent tick per minute, not jobs at fixed clock times (DR-0033).
- **Integrity rules:** skipping recommendations on a dirty tree; settlement and export at 17:30 ET.
- **Replay** (DR-0034): simulated times, full-universe tail boards skipped on idle days, `+dirty` allowed.
- **MOVEGEN-2 scope** (DR-0035): straddles at every strike, strangles from every OTM pair, RAND-1 sampling candidates uniformly.
- **Unchanged by design:** MKT-1 risk-neutral with the VRP adjustment off, HOLD-1, option volume end-of-day only.
- **Not built:** the text archive (DR-0036), which comes after Stop Point C.
