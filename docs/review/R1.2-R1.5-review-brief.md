## 1a. Changed since this brief was first written (2026-11-10/11)
MKT-1 is now **v3**. Read [DR-0037](../decisions/DR-0037-mkt1-v2.md) and [DR-0038](../decisions/DR-0038-arbitrage-free-svi.md); both are accepted.
- **Predicted mean anchored to market mids:** each leg's expected payoff is mid / DF.
- **Discount factor** = exp(−rT) from the board's rates; the forward comes from parity, `F = median(K + (C − P)/DF)`.
- **The fitted density** supplies only the distribution's shape, shifted to that mean and clipped.
- **The SVI fit** only accepts butterfly-free smiles (Durrleman's g ≥ 0 on k ∈ [−3, 3]).
- **RAND-1** is v3 and uses MKT-1 v3's forecasts.

Why: the August 2026 replay showed v1 ranking its own fit errors first. Its picks predicted +4% and realized −73%, and it rejected most expirations.

Attack in particular:
1. Can any candidate's predicted mean exceed carry?
2. Is the shift-and-clip of the distribution consistent with the recorded mean, quantiles and bins?
3. Can the rate interpolation (`discount_factor_from_rates`) read a rate that wasn't public at the board's `as_of`?
4. Is the Durrleman range wide enough for every grid MKT-1 builds?

## 3. Invariants to attack (most important first)

1. **No look-ahead in the engine.**
   - A decision (ASSIGN-1, an exit-policy call) may use only the decision board.
   - Fills may use only that slot's execution board, inside [decision + 5 min, decision + 15 min].
   - Settlement uses the official close, which is public later.
   - Can any path use a later board for an earlier decision, accept an execution board from the wrong slot, or record an event whose `event_time` comes before the data it used?
   - Check `_dividends_owed` and its ex-date window.
2. **Cash correctness.**
   - Realized P&L is the sum of every event's cash. Check the signs and amounts for:
     - entry legs and fees (per-leg fees must sum to the order's fees);
     - assignment strike cash;
     - the stock close and dividends owed;
     - the option exit;
     - settlement.
   - Construct a trade whose hand-computed P&L disagrees with `TradeSummary.realized_pnl`.
   - Any float in cash is a defect.
3. **State rebuilt from the journal** (`position_of`).
   - The journal is the only state. Can a valid event sequence produce the wrong open legs or stock?
   - Cases to try: two short legs assigned at different snapshots; an exit retried after the stock closed; settlement with stock left over.
   - Re-running a step must be a no-op, and a crash between `EXIT_SUBMITTED` and its fill must resume correctly. Can a re-run ever duplicate an event or skip one?
4. **FILL-1 semantics.**
   - All-or-none.
   - The full displayed size must cover each leg's quantity.
   - A single entry attempt; exits retry until expiration.
   - A missing execution snapshot means unfilled.
   - Closing trades reverse each leg's side.
5. **MKT-1 numerics.**
   - Parity forward and discount factor, implied-vol inversion, the SVI fit (`_inner` projection, Lee bound, Nelder–Mead), and the discrete Breeden–Litzenberger distribution with tail masses at conditional means.
   - Can a fit pass the arbitrage checks yet give clearly wrong expected payoffs?
   - Can any input crash `run_mkt1` instead of rejecting the expiration with a reason? Think about a NaN, a zero price, a single strike, or an expiration today.
   - Try to break the sanity property with a realistic chain.
6. **Determinism.**
   - The same board must give byte-identical recommendations and run logs, for both baselines.
   - Do RAND-1's draws depend on anything except `snapshot_id` and candidate hashes, such as dict or set order or the Python version?
   - Is the hash-order sampling actually uniform under the caps?
7. **Selection caps.**
   - K = 5, and one per underlying per baseline per ET day, across both snapshots (read from the registry).
   - Bug signals (predicted mean > +5%) are logged and never selected.

## 4. Already settled (don't re-report unless the implementation is wrong)
- Floats inside the model, with Decimal at the record boundary (DR-0030 §1).
- SVI rather than a spline; the $0.05 mid floor; bug signals excluded.
- **Mid-anchored mean, DF from rates, arbitrage-free fit** (DR-0037, DR-0038). These deliberately depart from handoff R1.4 steps 1 and 4–5, as approved by Josh.
- Stock closes immediately after an assumed assignment while the options retry; a missing underlying quote is annotated and skipped (DR-0029).
- RAND-1 uses MKT-1's forecasts (DR-0031, accepted).
- Not built yet: persisting the run logs, the official-close data adapter and the scheduler (all R1.6).
