Nine findings: **four high, five medium**. Reproductions are in `test_scheduler_review.py` and `test_reports_review.py`; all nine fail at their intended invariant assertions. The earlier review reports cover additional registry/export and execution crash defects that also affect this pipeline.

## F01 — Failed export pushes are not retried once the checkout is clean

- **Severity:** high.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/scheduler/ops.py:82`–`85`; completion marker at `src/remy_finance/scheduler/tick.py:243`–`244`.
- **What breaks:** `push_export()` commits successfully and then its push fails. The commit leaves a clean working tree. On the next tick the export has no new rows, and `push_export()` returns an empty string without retrying the unpushed commit. The caller marks the nightly export done. The remote external timestamp can therefore be absent even though the marker says the work completed. Later new data might incidentally push the commit, but recovery of this failed operation is not guaranteed.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_failed_export_push_is_retried_on_clean_checkout`. Use a real temporary bare Git remote and checkout, inject failure on the first push only, then retry normally. The remote branch remains at its old commit while local HEAD contains the export. No network or user repository is used.
- **Suggested fix:** Make push success independent of working-tree dirtiness. Commit only when needed, but retry pending local commits and verify remote acknowledgment before marking the operation complete.

## F02 — Open positions stop receiving management after leaving the monthly universe

- **Severity:** high.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/scheduler/pipeline.py:124`–`126`; capture universe at `src/remy_finance/scheduler/live.py:31`–`34` and replay universe in `scheduler/replay.py`.
- **What breaks:** Live and replay boards contain the current monthly membership. `Pipeline.manage()` skips an open trade whose underlying is absent. A name removed at the next monthly selection may remain absent through expiration, suppressing its assignment assessments and forced-exit retries. Settlement still eventually runs, but that is a different history and P&L from the required ASSIGN-1 behavior.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_existing_position_is_managed_after_symbol_leaves_universe`. Open the normal XYZ spread on October 30 with November 20 expiration. Supply the November 2 decision/execution boards for a new universe containing ABC. A spy confirms that management is never called for the still-open XYZ trade.
- **Suggested fix:** Capture and persist the data needed to manage all outstanding positions independently of eligibility for new recommendations. Keep the new-trade selection universe unchanged, and preserve point-in-time/execution-slot rules for management data. Do not simply admit old holdings to new selections by expanding their universe silently.

## F03 — Recommendation errors prevent independent execution capture and position management

- **Severity:** high.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/scheduler/tick.py:208`; session-wide guard at `:129`, dependent steps at `:215`–`229`.
- **What breaks:** Only `DirtyTreeError` is handled within the recommendation step. A baseline or recommendation error unwinds the entire session, before execution capture, fills, and management. Each subsequent tick retries recommendations first and fails at the same place. Healthy capture and management work can consequently be blocked for the whole window, including management of older trades. The execution slot may also remain unresolved because its gap logic is never reached.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_recommendation_failure_does_not_block_execution_or_management`. Inject a failing recommendation step, while real synthetic capture remains healthy. Run at 10:00 and 10:07. The decision is stored, but no execution snapshot exists and management is never reached.
- **Suggested fix:** Guard recommendation generation separately from execution capture and management. Preserve any legitimate partially submitted orders for crash recovery, record the failure, and continue steps that do not require successful new recommendations. Only mark a session complete after its required work has actually resolved.

## F04 — EVAL-1's 61-day eligibility rule is displayed but not applied

- **Severity:** high.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/reports/render.py:236`; activity-only eligibility count at `:287`–`299`.
- **What breaks:** `group_stats()` calculates forecast scores and returns over every closed filled trade. `eval1_eligible()` is used only to display a count. Recent early-closing trades therefore enter the EVAL-1 analytical cohort despite the frozen cutoff rule, biasing the sample toward early assignments/exits. Rank analyses also use all closed trades.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_cumulative_forecast_scores_apply_61_day_censoring`. The existing end-to-end fixture has zero eligible closed trades at the chosen cutoff. The report nevertheless scores four MKT-1 and three RAND-1 trades.
- **Suggested fix:** Retain all-record activity/machinery counts, but form a separate return/forecast/rank analysis cohort with decisions at least 61 calendar days old and trades closed before cutoff. If showing uncensored operational results too, label them separately rather than presenting them as EVAL-1 scores.

## F05 — A restart on a later date never records the previous day's missed slots

- **Severity:** medium.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/scheduler/tick.py:120`–`130`.
- **What breaks:** The scheduler resolves only today's sessions. If it runs before Monday's open, is asleep or stopped through all Monday captures, and resumes Tuesday morning, none of Monday's four slots becomes a gap. They remain neither stored nor gap indefinitely, contrary to slot finality and the runbook's assurance that sleep produces recorded gaps.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_missing_previous_day_slots_are_recorded_after_restart`. Run Monday at 09:00, then Tuesday at 09:00. All four Monday `(session, kind)` combinations are still missing from `gaps()`.
- **Suggested fix:** Reconcile overdue sessions since the last completed scheduler watermark on startup/tick. Preserve existing snapshots and gaps; record only missing slots. Never fetch historical data to fill a missed prospective slot.

## F06 — PIT randomizes at a unique quantile without evidence of a point mass

- **Severity:** medium.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/reports/scores.py:56`–`65`.
- **What breaks:** Any equality with a recorded quantile triggers a draw between neighboring quantile levels. For a strictly increasing interpolated quantile grid, a unique equality is an interpolation knot, not a demonstrated CDF jump. The PIT becomes seed-dependent where it should be the known quantile probability. The existing unit test encodes this erroneous equality-as-jump behavior.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_unique_quantile_is_interpolated_without_an_invented_mass`. Levels `[0.1, 0.5, 0.9]`, values `[-1, 0, 1]`, realized value `0`, seed `trade-a`: expected PIT `0.5`; actual approximately `0.39661960245`.
- **Suggested fix:** Preserve singleton exact interpolation knots. Randomize only where a supported point mass exists and in the separately specified outer-tail cases, using justified mass boundaries.

## F07 — Reports omit the commits that produced the scored engine records

- **Severity:** medium.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/reports/render.py:38`–`55`.
- **What breaks:** Headers identify the evaluation-protocol commit and registry heads but omit `Recommendation.code_commit`. EVAL-1 §7 requires the code commit of every scored engine. Engine/version grouping is correct, but it does not identify all implementations represented within a version.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_cumulative_report_identifies_scored_engine_commits`. The real integration fixture contains recommendations with commit `abcdef0123`; the report never includes it.
- **Suggested fix:** Include every represented engine/version/code-commit combination in report provenance. Preserve multiple commits if a version group contains more than one; do not substitute the reporting code or protocol commit.

## F08 — Required descriptive CRPS bootstrap intervals are absent

- **Severity:** medium.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/reports/render.py:364`–`369`.
- **What breaks:** The report prints mean CRPS without EVAL-1 §2.1's cluster-bootstrap interval. No implementation exists in `reports/` for §4's specified 10,000-resample, expiration-week bootstrap. The prohibition on interim P1/P2 p-values does not prohibit or waive the specified descriptive uncertainty intervals.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_cumulative_crps_includes_required_cluster_bootstrap_interval`. Generate the integration report with a fully eligible cutoff. Its forecast-score section contains neither an interval nor an insufficient-clusters explanation.
- **Suggested fix:** Implement the frozen cluster bootstrap and fixed seed, reporting intervals when estimable and an explicit insufficient-clusters result otherwise. Continue withholding interim confirmatory P1/P2 p-values.

## F09 — The recorded underlying distributions are never evaluated

- **Severity:** medium.
- **File and line:** `src/remy_finance/reports/render.py:282`–`287`.
- **What breaks:** EVAL-1 §2.2 requires CRPS and continuous log scores for the recorded risk-neutral `S_T/F` forecasts against expiration official closes. `cumulative_report()` consumes only recommendations/journal, and no underlying-distribution scoring path exists elsewhere in `reports/`. The persisted `ExpirationFit.rn_quantiles` prerequisite is present but unused for evaluation.
- **Minimal reproduction:** `test_cumulative_includes_underlying_return_forecast_scores`. Confirm that the integration fixture has nonempty persisted risk-neutral quantiles and settled expirations, then generate the report. It contains no underlying-distribution evaluation.
- **Suggested fix:** Supply the persisted fits and point-in-time official closes to reporting, and display the §2.2 metrics separately from strategy-return scores. Apply clear outcome-availability and cutoff rules to those observations.
