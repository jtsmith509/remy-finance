# DR-0037: MKT-1 v2: mid-anchored mean, discount factor from rates; redo the replay month

- **Status:** accepted (Josh, 2026-11-10: anchor the mean to market mids; discount factor from rates; archive the first replay and re-run it)
- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Source:** the August 2026 replay (`data/reports/replay-2026-08-cumulative.md`, `power-inputs-2026-08.md`) and `remy replay diagnose --date 2026-08-03 --session open`. Amends [DR-0030](DR-0030-mkt1-details.md) §2 and §6. Departs from handoff R1.4 steps 1 and 4–5, as approved by Josh. Affects RAND-1's forecasts ([DR-0031](DR-0031-rand1-details.md)).
- **Stage:** R1, before the 5-day live run

## What the replay showed
1. **MKT-1 selected its own fit errors.**
   - v1 took each candidate's predicted mean from the fitted SVI and Breeden–Litzenberger density. On real chains the fit misvalues cheap legs by a few percent, more than the half-spread on liquid names (QQQ's are well under 1%).
   - Ranking by that mean therefore put first the candidates the fit overvalued most. In every structure group the highest prediction sat at +4.4% to +5.0%, just under the +5% bug-signal cut.
   - **Outcome:** MKT-1's 173 closed trades predicted **+4.2%** on average and realized **−73.5%** (median −100%). 35% of realized returns fell below the forecast's 1% quantile; the PIT histogram was U-shaped; predicted versus realized had Spearman −0.12.
   - On synthetic chains, where the model is exact, the best prediction is +0.1%: the code was right, and the method is what failed on real data.
2. **91% of expirations were rejected.** On the 3 August open board only 32 of 376 fits succeeded; **237** were rejected for a parity-implied discount factor outside [0.90, 1.0001]. On short-dated chains the parity slope is noisy (about ±0.002 on QQQ's $1 strikes), so the 1.0001 cap threw out good fits. Only 26,924 of 423,057 candidates were scored.
3. **The run used MOVEGEN-1.** It started before the MOVEGEN-2 amendment was pulled: none of its 420 recommendations was a new long-volatility type. So it doesn't represent the live configuration.

## Decisions
1. **Mid-anchored mean.**
   - Each leg's expected terminal payoff is its **quoted mid carried to expiration** (mid / DF). The predicted mean is then `(carry − half-spreads − fees) / capital at risk` **by construction**: the handoff's sanity property.
   - The fitted density still supplies the distribution's **shape** (the 15 quantiles, tail measures and EVAL-1 bins). Its return distribution is shifted to the anchored mean and clipped to the attainable range.
   - With mids anchoring the mean, a prediction above +5% can now only come from a crossed or stale quote. The bug-signal rule (log and exclude) is unchanged.
   - **Consequence:** MKT-1 now ranks the cheapest structures to trade, those with the smallest spread cost relative to capital at risk. That is what a market-implied baseline should do. EVAL-1's P1 (unbiased forecasts) becomes a test of the market's own pricing plus costs.
2. **Discount factor from rates.**
   - `DF = exp(−r·T)`, with r the board's latest point-in-time rate per tenor (DR-0011: SOFR, 1M, 3M, 6M and 1Y), interpolated linearly in maturity, flat beyond the ends.
   - A board without a rate rejects the expiration as `NO_RATE`.
   - The **forward still comes from parity**: `F = median over near-the-money pairs of K + (C_mid − P_mid) / DF`. That keeps the handoff's reason for using parity, which is that no dividend forecasts are needed.
   - The DF bounds are removed from the config.
3. **Versions.** MKT-1 and RAND-1 go to **engine version 2**, which changes their artifact hashes and trade IDs. v1 records are never pooled with v2 (EVAL-1 §7).
4. **Redo the replay month.**
   - `remy replay archive --label 2026-08-movegen1-mkt1v1` moves the replay registry and replay run logs to `data/archive/<label>/`. Nothing is deleted: the archived registry still verifies. Stored boards stay in place.
   - August is then re-run with MOVEGEN-2 and MKT-1/RAND-1 v2 on those boards. The power-estimate addendum uses the re-run.
   - This follows the R0 precedent for replay data. A prospective record is never archived or replaced.

## Also added
- `remy replay diagnose`: predicted means by structure type, split by whether legs lie inside the fitted strike range, plus the top predictions and the fit rejections by reason.
- `remy replay bench`: timing on a real board. On the Mac mini (3 August open, 167k quotes): **movegen 31 s, 423,057 candidates, scoring 4 s, recommendation step about 31 s.** That is well inside the live budget.
- Earlier the same day: the performance fixes (cached board and candidate hashes, a single scoring pass, registry read caches). They cut the replay's per-board overhead from minutes to seconds.

## Tests (489 passing)
- **Mid-anchored mean:** for every scored candidate the mean equals `(Σ mid/DF − premium − fees)/CaR`, and with spreads it never exceeds carry.
- **A mispriced quote** (a 115C at 0.01) no longer produces a high prediction.
- **The bug-signal path** still logs and excludes.
- **DF from rates** reproduces the synthetic model's DF exactly, and no rate means `NO_RATE`.
- **The forward from parity** recovers the model's forward.
- **The shifted distribution** stays monotone and above −100%, and its bins sum to exactly 1.
- **The archive command** moves only the replay registry and run logs, keeps the boards, never overwrites, and a fresh replay reuses the boards.
